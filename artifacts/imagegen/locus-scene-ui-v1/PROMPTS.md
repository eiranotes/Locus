# ImageGen prompts

All five prompts supplied the existing `locus-art-v1` and
`locus-collection-expansion-v1` contact sheets as strict style references. Every
atlas requested a perfectly flat `#FF00FF` background, a 5 by 2 grid with no
visible dividers, crisp nearest-neighbor pixel art, deep navy outlines, restrained
Locus colors, one isolated subject per cell, and no text, labels, logos, people,
or unrelated scenery.

## Terrain A

Row-major subjects: smooth pebble trio; moss patch; three grass blades; clover
patch; two tiny mushrooms; scattered autumn leaves; tiny wildflower tuft; fallen
twig; small fern; square iron drain grate.

## Terrain B

Row-major subjects: cracked cobble mark; tiny chalk star marking; shallow puddle
glint; single wet leaf; small snow tuft; amber dry weed; pair of acorns;
scattered flower petals; three tiny stepping-stone marks; grass sprouting from a
crack.

## Atmosphere

Row-major subjects: rain ripple cluster; curled mist wisp; frost sparkle cluster;
three warm floating motes; windblown leaf pair; dawn pink sparkle; pale morning
ray fragments; evening amber window-light motes; night moth pair; compact falling
raindrop cluster.

## Placement editor

Row-major subjects: hollow mint isometric target; amber selected target; coral
invalid target; cream grab-hand cursor; mint placement chevron; left, up, down,
and right arrows; amber quarter-turn rotate arrow.

## Actions

Row-major subjects: capture jar; hammer and blueprint; placement grid and pin;
rotate arrow; storage crate; weather cloud and star; surrounding leaf and pebble;
visitor lantern; open collection book; settings gear.
