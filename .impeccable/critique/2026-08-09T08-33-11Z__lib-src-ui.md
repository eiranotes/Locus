## Design thesis

**A quiet playable shelf.** The neighborhood is the workspace, inventory is a
collection drawer, crafting is a workbench, and the codex is a field guide.
The shell should be productive and restrained while the generated objects,
materials, visitors, and atmosphere carry the personality.

Keep the existing navy, cream, mint, amber, blue, and violet palette and native
Korean system typography. Mint denotes action/selection, amber denotes
discovery/reward, and weather colors carry material state. Use Material icons
for navigation and utilities, not as substitutes for collectible art.

## Priority direction

### P1 — Make home scene-first and persistent

The v6 contract makes the diorama the dominant 60–70% surface, but
`home_screen.dart` currently places title, resource badges, visitor card, and
other cards around a square scene. Compress resources into one strip, move the
single visitor/construction goal into the scene frame, remove the dashboard-like
summary pair, and derive the visible visitor from persisted sightings after the
arrival dialog clears.

Affected files: `lib/src/ui/screens/home_screen.dart`,
`lib/src/diorama/diorama_view.dart`, and
`lib/src/app/app_controller_actions.dart`.

### P1 — Make placement spatially truthful

Logical grid axes appear as isometric diagonals. A horizontal left/up/down/right
row makes a valid move look wrong. The current slice changes it to a 2×2
diagonal pad with 48 dp targets, preserves validation-before-mutation, and
announces selected catalog entries. Next add a concise board legend and scene
semantics before direct drag.

Affected files: `lib/src/ui/widgets/placement_direction_pad.dart`,
`lib/src/ui/screens/placement_editor_screen.dart`, and
`lib/src/diorama/diorama_view.dart`.

### P1 — Make crafting a workbench, not a form

The current slice adds recipe-specific 25%, 60%, and 85% construction art and
shows the result predicted from available steps. Weather and surroundings stay
selectable material tokens. Next centralize complete sprite composition so the
same weather surface and footprint layers render identically in crafting,
inventory, codex, diorama, and sharing.

Affected files: `lib/src/ui/screens/crafting_screen.dart`,
`lib/src/ui/widgets/object_visual_preview.dart`, and
`lib/src/diorama/object_renderer.dart`.

### P2 — Separate collection action from navigation

The bottom shell mixes three destinations with the foreground collection
action. Keep navigation destinations stable; move collection into the scene
action layer as a labeled, stateful control. Show the ready-channel count in
its semantics.

Affected file: `lib/src/ui/app_shell.dart`.

### P2 — Adapt secondary collections

Record and visitor grids, material pickers, and the placement catalog use fixed
geometry. Add compact/medium/expanded policies, one-column large-text fallbacks,
and a navigation rail only on expanded Android widths. Preserve the phone-first
iOS composition.

Affected files: `lib/src/ui/screens/inventory_screen.dart`,
`lib/src/ui/screens/codex_screen.dart`,
`lib/src/ui/screens/crafting_screen.dart`, and `lib/src/ui/app_shell.dart`.

## Asset composition decision

Do not author every craft combination. Ten recipes × six weather kinds × five
surroundings choices already equals 300, and direction/time expansion reaches
3,600 visual states. The maintained composition is:

```text
recipe/direction base
+ shared weather surface treatment
+ shared weather footprint effect
+ runtime time palette
+ runtime surroundings connector
+ deterministic seeded detail
+ recipe construction/complete state
+ native place plaque
```

Current bounded packages:

- 40 completed directional bases: 10 recipes × 4 directions;
- 30 construction sprites: 10 recipes × 3 progress stages;
- 12 weather treatments: 6 surface patterns + 6 footprint effects;
- native/code-rendered plaque remains planned because Korean place text must
  never be baked into a bitmap.

The construction pack is live in the crafting/inventory preview path. Weather
treatments are generated, processed, validated, and cataloged, but compositor
integration is intentionally deferred until preview and scene sprite
composition share one alpha-clipped layer resolver.

## Platform and accessibility requirements

- Pressed feedback must paint above tappable card surfaces; selected state must
  be announced separately from decorative highlighting.
- Add a concise semantic summary for the diorama and editor live position,
  direction, valid anchors, and move result.
- Keep iOS targets at least 44 pt and Android targets at least 48 dp.
- Respect large text, increased contrast, and `MediaQuery.disableAnimations`.
- Use 150–220 ms state transitions and existing haptics; no ornamental page
  choreography.

Questions skipped: the owner explicitly selected the game-tone, catalog-driven
asset direction and asked implementation to continue now.
